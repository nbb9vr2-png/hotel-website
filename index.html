Good to know

All apartments include a fully equipped kitchen (refrigerator, microwave, IH cooktop, kettle, dishes and utensils), a washing machine and dryer, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV, and premium Kyoto-made bedding and toiletries. Towels and linens are provided.
Yes — we offer 10% off for 7-night stays and 20% off for stays of 30 nights or more. These rates are automatically applied when you book via our website. Contact us directly for custom long-term arrangements.
Absolutely. NOMA is designed with families in mind. We can provide portable cots, high chairs, and children's tableware upon request at no extra charge. Our apartments give children room to play, and the neighborhood is safe and walkable.
Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Self check-in is available 24/7 via a digital key. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged (subject to availability) — please request when booking.
Our guest services team is available by phone, chat, and email from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily. We speak English, Japanese, and Chinese. We're happy to help with restaurant recommendations, transport, day trips, and anything else you need.
NOMA is located in central Nakagyo Ward. Nijo Castle is a 10-minute walk. Nishiki Market is 8 minutes. Gion is a 15-minute cycle. Arashiyama Bamboo Grove is 20 minutes by train. Kyoto Station is 12 minutes by subway.
